I have also reviewed the latest version of the SAP. I've replied to some of Karla's comments directly in the document and added several comments of my own, mainly regarding the statistical methods, where I would appreciate your input and feedback.
The main points I've raised are:
- Length of stay: The choice of negative binomial regression, whether length of stay is recorded as integer counts or continuous durations, and whether a gamma model might be more appropriate. I've also raised questions about the clinical interpretation when stays end due to death or transfer.
- Missing data: Whether the proposed 10% threshold should determine the choice between available-case analysis and multiple imputation, and whether the missing-data strategy for secondary outcomes should depend on missingness in the primary outcome.
- Model specification and inference: Small-sample corrections, criteria for acceptable model convergence, and whether adjustment for cluster size should be included in the primary model.
- Repeated measurements: How potential repeated ED presentations from the same patient should be handled (is it even an issue?), and whether QoL and disability outcomes will be analysed separately at each follow-up time point.
- Subgroup analyses: Particularly geographical region, given the potentially small number of clusters per state and the need to prespecify subgroup categories.
- Sensitivity analyses: Whether we should prioritise and potentially reduce the number of planned analyses, as Karla also suggested.
- I've also added a few comments regarding intercurrent events, correlation measures, and outcome definitions.

All comments are included directly in the Word document. Please have a look when you have a chance and let me know your thoughts!
